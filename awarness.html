Awareness Programs

Together we can create a healthier, greener and happier society through education, community service and environmental protection.

🌱 Environment Protection

Plant trees, reduce plastic usage, recycle waste and keep our surroundings clean. Every tree planted helps protect our future.

☀️ Clean Energy Awareness

Promote solar panels, energy-efficient appliances, LED lighting and renewable sources of energy for a sustainable future.

🍛 Zero Hunger Mission

Organize food donation drives and distribute nutritious meals to families facing food insecurity.

📚 Education For Every Child

Provide free education, books, uniforms, scholarships and digital learning opportunities to underprivileged children.

👴 Elderly Care Program

Support senior citizens with healthcare, emotional care, food assistance and recreational activities.

👶 Child Welfare

Ensure every child receives proper nutrition, education, healthcare and protection from exploitation.

🐄 Goshala Welfare

Protect and care for rescued cows by providing shelter, medical treatment, nutritious food and regular maintenance.

💧 Water Conservation

Spread awareness about rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge and responsible use of water resources.

🩺 Free Medical Camps

Organize regular health camps offering free checkups, medicines, eye testing and blood donation drives.

🌳 Tree Plantation Drive

Encourage schools, colleges and communities to participate in tree plantation activities to increase green cover.

♻️ Waste Management

Educate citizens about waste segregation, recycling and composting to reduce environmental pollution.

🤝 Community Volunteering

Inspire people to volunteer in food distribution, education, disaster relief and environmental conservation activities.
