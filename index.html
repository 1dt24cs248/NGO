About Vedic Aashram

Spreading peace, knowledge, and service to humanity.

About Us

Vedic Aashram is a spiritual and social service organization dedicated to preserving ancient Vedic traditions while serving society. We provide spiritual education, meditation, and support to people seeking peace and self-growth.

Our Vision

To build a peaceful society guided by spiritual wisdom, moral values, and compassion, where every individual lives in harmony with nature and humanity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread Vedic knowledge, promote meditation and yoga, support the needy, and provide free spiritual education to people from all walks of life.

Our Team

We have 35+ dedicated staff members including monks, teachers, volunteers, and social workers who work together to run the Ashram, serve devotees, and help the community.
